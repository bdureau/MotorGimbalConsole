Motor Gimbal Console Application Settings

This guide describes how to configure the global settings and behavior of the Bear Altimeter Motor Gimbal Android console.

Note: Changes made here apply to the mobile application environment and communication interfaces, not to the rocket flight computer firmware parameters.

Communication & Connection Type

Default Connection Interface: Select your primary physical communication channel: Bluetooth (RFCOMM) or USB-OTG Serial.

Baud Rate: Configure the transmission rate for ground telemetry (default is typically 38400 or 57600 baud depending on your transceiver module).

Telemetry Modules: Configure external telemetry radios such as 3DR Radio modules, LoRa E32, or LoRa E220 transceivers for extended ground-to-rocket range.

Display & Units

Unit System: Switch between Metric units (meters, m/s, °C) and Imperial units (feet, ft/s, °F) for altitude charts and real-time dashboard gauges.

Theme & Appearance: Customize application colors, night/dark mode for outdoor sunlight readability, and graphical plot curve smoothing.

Voice & Audio Telemetry

Speech Annunciations (TTS): Enable or disable real-time voice callouts during launch, including current altitude, apogee detection, and low battery alarms.

Annunciation Interval: Define the audio refresh frequency (e.g., every 50m of ascent or every 1 second).
