Select Bluetooth Module

This dialog allows you to choose the target wireless Bluetooth transceiver when connecting to your Bear Altimeter Motor Gimbal for the first time or when switching between different rockets on the field.

Pairing & Setup Procedure

  1. Android System Pairing: Before selecting a module in the app, you must pair the Bluetooth transceiver (HC-05, HC-06, or HC-07) in your device's Settings > Bluetooth menu. The default PIN is usually 1234 or 0000.
  2. Device Identification: In the paired devices list, tap the Bluetooth adapter mounted on your current rocket gimbal.
  3. Establish Connection: Return to the main console screen and tap Connect. If the wrong device is selected or the flight controller is powered off, the connection handshake will fail.
⚠ IMPORTANT ADVICE
Rename each paired Bluetooth module within your Android Bluetooth settings (e.g., "Rocket-Gimbal-1", "Rocket-Gimbal-2"). This prevents sending tuning parameters or commands to the wrong rocket on the launch pad.

Troubleshooting Connection Issues

  • Verify that the Bluetooth module LED is blinking (waiting for connection) or steady (linked).
  • Ensure the gimbal avionics battery is charged and that power is supplied to the transceiver module.
  • If the device does not appear in the list, verify that location/nearby devices permissions are granted for this application.
