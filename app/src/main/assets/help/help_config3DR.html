3DR Radio Telemetry Module Configuration

To achieve extended, long-range telemetry links beyond Bluetooth limits, you can replace your wireless Bluetooth adapter with a 3DR radio telemetry transceiver pair.

Power Note: Ensure your Android device provides sufficient USB-OTG power output to drive the ground radio module. High-power radio modules (e.g., 500mW or 1W) may require a powered USB hub or external battery pack to avoid unexpected disconnects.

Required Hardware for Configuration

  • A USB-to-TTL serial adapter cable.
  • A USB-OTG adapter suitable for your phone or tablet (USB-C or Micro-USB).
  • A matching JST connector adapter and JST wiring harness.

Baud Rate Compatibility

Most commercial 3DR radio modules arrive factory configured for 57600 baud. However, the default serial speed of the rocket Gimbal controller is 38400 baud. You must reconfigure both radio modules so that their serial interface matches the Gimbal flight computer baud rate.

⚠ CRITICAL PRECAUTION
Never disconnect or unplug the radio module while reading or writing configuration data, as this may corrupt the module's EEPROM settings.

Configuration Steps

  1. Connect Hardware: Connect the 3DR radio to your Android device using the USB-to-TTL adapter and the OTG connector.
  2. Read Configuration: Tap [Retrieve cfg]. The application will scan through multiple standard baud rates until it detects and connects to the module, then loads its current parameters into the fields.
  3. Modify Settings: Adjust the desired parameters (RF frequency channels, air data rate, net ID, and serial baud rate matching your Gimbal board).
  4. Write Configuration: Tap [Save cfg] to burn the settings into the module. If any communication error is detected during write operations, the save process will abort automatically.
Paired Transceivers: 3DR radios operate in matched pairs (Air module on the rocket and Ground module on the console). You must repeat this configuration procedure for both modules, ensuring identical Net IDs, frequency bands, and matching serial speeds.
