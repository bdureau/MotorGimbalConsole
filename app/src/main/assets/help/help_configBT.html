Bluetooth Module Configuration

To connect your rocket altimeter or motor gimbal controller to your Android phone, the Bluetooth transceiver must be configured to operate at the exact same serial baud rate as the flight computer board.

This utility programs commercial Bluetooth modules such as the HC-05, HC-06, or compatible transceivers.

Required Programming Hardware

  • A USB-to-TTL serial adapter cable.
  • A USB-OTG adapter suitable for your Android phone or tablet (USB-C or Micro-USB).
  • A JST connector adapter and wiring harness.

Baud Rate Alignment

Most commercial Bluetooth modules arrive from the factory preset to 9600 baud. However, your altimeter and gimbal board communicates by default at 38400 baud. You must reconfigure the module's internal UART speed to 38400 baud so that flight data packets can be transmitted successfully.

⚠ CRITICAL PRECAUTION
Do not disconnect or unplug the module during the read or write process, as doing so may corrupt its internal firmware or AT register settings.

Configuration Procedure

  1. Hardware Connection: Connect your Bluetooth module to your Android device using the USB-to-TTL adapter cable and OTG connector.
  2. Module Type: Select your specific module model from the dropdown list (e.g., HC-05 or HC-06). If you are uncertain, keep the default option.
  3. Retrieve Settings: Tap [Retrieve cfg]. The app will attempt to contact the module at the target speed (38400 baud) and will automatically scan other standard speeds (9600, 19200, 57600, 115200) until a valid AT response is received.
  4. Modify Parameters: Rename the device (e.g., "Rocket-Gimbal-1") and ensure the new baud rate is set to 38400 baud. You may also set a custom PIN code (default: 1234).
  5. Save Settings: Tap [Save cfg] to write the new configuration into the module's memory. If an error occurs, the process will abort safely.
Note: Repeat this configuration procedure for each Bluetooth transceiver you intend to use with your rocket avionics.
