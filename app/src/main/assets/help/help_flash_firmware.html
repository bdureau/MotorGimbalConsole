Firmware Flashing Guide

This guide explains how to flash or recover the firmware on your Motor Gimbal controller or STM32-based rocket altimeter using your Android device.

Compatibility: The flight computer firmware version must match the communication protocol of this console application to enable configuration and telemetry streaming.

Required Hardware

  • A USB-to-TTL serial adapter cable.
  • A USB-OTG adapter suitable for your Android phone or tablet (USB-C or Micro-USB).
  • JST wiring harness for serial RX/TX/GND connection.

Supported Flight Hardware

Ensure you identify your hardware revision correctly before selecting a firmware binary:

Gimbal Controller (MPU6050 6-DOF IMU)

Gimbal Controller (BNO055 Absolute Orientation Sensor)

⚠ CAUTION
Flashing an incompatible firmware binary will cause sensor read failures or completely prevent the gimbal board from operating. Never disconnect the USB cable during an active flash procedure.

Standard vs. Recovery Firmware

  • Standard Firmware: Use for regular firmware updates to access new flight features or protocol improvements.
  • Recovery Firmware: Use if EEPROM data is corrupted, causing boot loops or blocking serial connections. After flashing the recovery binary to erase bad parameters, reflash the standard firmware image.

Flashing Procedure (STM32 Flight Computers)

  1. Hardware Connection: Connect the USB-TTL adapter to the flight computer and plug the OTG connector into your Android device.
  2. Select Firmware: Choose the firmware target corresponding to your hardware board, or use automatic board detection.
  3. Set Bootloader Mode: Move the BOOT0 jumper to the right (programming position).
  4. Reset Board: Press and release the hardware Reset button on the flight board.
  5. Flash Binary: Tap [Flash Firmware]. Wait until the progress bar reaches 100%.
  6. Normal Boot Mode: Move the BOOT0 jumper back to the left (normal run position).
  7. Finalize: Press the Reset button once more to boot into the newly flashed firmware.
