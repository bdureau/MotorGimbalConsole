Motor Gimbal Configuration Guide

This guide explains how to calibrate and tune the thrust vector control (TVC) motorized gimbal parameters on your Bear Altimeter flight computer.

Connection Requirement: Ensure your mobile device is connected via Bluetooth or USB-OTG and that the flight computer is powered on before modifying parameters.

Servo Calibration & Limits

Servo Neutral / Center Trim (X / Y): Adjusts the mechanical center of the motor mount so the motor thrust axis is perfectly aligned with the rocket's longitudinal centerline.

Servo Travel Limits (Min / Max): Sets the maximum physical deflection angle in degrees for both pitch and yaw axes. Restrict these limits to prevent mechanical binding or linkage collisions.

PID Stabilization Loop

Proportional Gain (Kp): Dictates the corrective torque proportional to the measured attitude error. Higher values stiffen stabilization, but excessive Kp causes rapid oscillation.

Integral Gain (Ki): Compensates for steady-state offset, aerodynamic weathercocking, or structural asymmetry over time.

Derivative Gain (Kd): Dampens the angular velocity and resists sudden rotational motion to eliminate overshoot.

Sensor Orientation & Flight Timing

IMU Mounting Orientation: Defines board rotation relative to the rocket airframe (Yaw, Pitch, Roll axis orientation).

Gimbal Active Duration: Specifies the active stabilization window following motor ignition. Stabilization typically deactivates automatically upon motor burnout.

⚠ CRITICAL SAFETY STEP
Always verify servo response on the launch pad by tilting the airframe manually. The gimbal must deflect in the direction that counters the tilt angle.

Reading & Writing Parameters

  • Tap [Read Config] to query the active settings from the flight board EEPROM.
  • Tap [Write Config] to save adjustments. Wait for confirmation before power cycling.
