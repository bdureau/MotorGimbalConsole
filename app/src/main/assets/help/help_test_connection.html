Connection Reliability Test

This utility diagnoses telemetry link quality and communication integrity between your Android console and the rocket flight computer, which is particularly useful when deploying long-range 3DR radio modems or LoRa modules.

Usage Tip: Perform this link quality test on the launch pad prior to flight to confirm signal stability and verify RF link margin at distance.

Monitored Link Metrics

Packets Sent / Received: Total count of telemetry test frames exchanged across the active serial link.

Dropped Packets: The count of lost or unacknowledged data packets caused by RF interference, antenna misalignment, or excessive range.

Packet Loss Rate (%): Percentage of lost frames relative to total transmissions. A loss rate below 2% is ideal for uninterrupted live telemetry.

Interpreting Test Results

  • Loss rate < 2%: Link quality is excellent; telemetry and audio callouts will operate reliably.
  • Loss rate between 2% and 10%: Acceptable link, but you may experience brief delays in live dashboard charts.
  • Loss rate > 10%: Poor link quality. Check antenna orientation, inspect cable connectors, or increase transmitter output power before flight.
⚠ PRE-FLIGHT RECOMMENDATION
Do not launch if packet loss is consistently high on the pad. High packet loss often indicates an antenna polarization mismatch, nearby RF interference, or an underpowered transceiver.
